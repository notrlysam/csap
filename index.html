    How this was worked out

    The data

    Real readings from three CSAP weather stations, 11 Aug to 29 Sep 2026. Each station reports temperature, humidity, wind, sunshine and rain every 5 or 10 minutes. We use daily summaries. A day is only used when at least 90% of its readings arrived.

    The calculation

    1. Work out how much water a crop gives off to the air each day (ETo), using the international FAO-56 Penman-Monteith method.

    ETo = (0.408·Δ·Rn + γ·900/(T+273)·u₂·VPD) / (Δ + γ·(1+0.34·u₂))

    2. Daily balance = rain minus ETo.

    3. Add up the last 7 days. This is the number behind the colour.

    The colours

    Too dry 7-day balance below −15 mm
    Just right between −15 and +15 mm
    Too wet above +15 mm
    Black pod Rising once wet. High after 3 wet days in a row, or a week above +50 mm.

    15 mm is roughly 4 days of the crop's normal water use (about 3.5 mm a day here).

    Checked against the weather

    • ETo is between 1.3 and 5.1 mm a day at all three stations.
    • Gran Couva's 70.7 mm storm on 12 Aug shows as Too wet for 7 days.
    • Rio Claro (47 mm) and Maracas Valley (40 mm) late-September storms turn Too wet.
    • Maracas Valley is Too dry through most of early September.

    What we cannot say yet

    • Only three places have a station. Nothing here applies to farms elsewhere.
    • Some days are missing (for example 10 to 14 Sep) because too few readings arrived.
    • Wind readings are very low (often under 0.3 m/s). We need to know if that is real or a sensor limit.
    • No soil moisture probe to confirm the balance.
